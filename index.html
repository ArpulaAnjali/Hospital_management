About Our Hospital

Caring for the Community Since 2010

CarePlus Hospital is a premier multi-specialty healthcare institution dedicated to providing top-notch medical services. With a team of highly qualified doctors, trained nursing staff, and modern diagnostic technologies, we ensure that every patient receives compassionate and comprehensive care.

We pride ourselves on clinical excellence and patient safety. Our patient-centric approach ensures you receive standard care from consultation through recovery.

Key Services

24/7 Emergency

Round-the-clock emergency support with fully equipped ambulances and trauma care specialists.

Cardiology

Complete heart care including diagnostics, therapy, and specialized coronary surgical procedures.

Pediatrics

Specialized physical and emotional care for infants, kids, and teens in a comfortable setting.

Neurology

Expert diagnostic tests and treatment for chronic nerve, spine, and brain disorders.
